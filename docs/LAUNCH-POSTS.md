## Honest-tone tips

- Say it is **early**. It builds trust and invites the corrections you want.
- Ask teachers and scholars **by name** to review a lesson; people respond to a specific request.
- Post the **question**, not the answer ("Can you guess what *namaste* literally means?"). The generated daily posts already do this.
- Don't promise "every day" forever. Something like "every day this month" is easier to keep, and you can extend it.
